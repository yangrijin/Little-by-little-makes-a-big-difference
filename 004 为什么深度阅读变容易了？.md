Deep reading becomes easier whenever an environment reduces the amount of executive control needed to resist competing demands, regardless of whether that environment contains natural elements. Nature is one effective way to achieve this, but structured environments such as airplanes and high-speed trains can achieve a similar outcome by minimizing distractions, reducing decision-making, and creating a temporary separation from everyday responsibilities.
每当环境能减少抵御各种干扰需求所需的执行控制力时，深度阅读就会变得更容易，无论该环境是否包含自然元素。大自然是实现这一目标的一种有效途径，但飞机和高速列车等结构化环境也能通过最大限度地减少干扰、降低决策负担，以及暂时让人脱离日常职责，从而达到类似的效果。
